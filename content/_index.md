      title: About the lab
      text: |
        SAIL works from a societal problem to a deployed system. We build the language data a problem needs, do the research, turn the results into intelligent systems and applications, and work with partners to deploy and measure their impact.

        Advances in language technology should not be limited to benchmarks and publications. They should help people access information, make institutions more responsive, amplify underrepresented voices, and improve public communication.

        **Research.** New methods, datasets, models, and evaluation approaches in NLP, AI, and language intelligence.

        **Build.** Prototypes, platforms, tools, and applications that address real-world problems.

        **Impact.** Work with communities, institutions, and organizations to evaluate and deploy technology where it creates social value.
