SAIL explores how AI and NLP can be applied to online safety, health information, climate change, civic engagement, education, and language accessibility. We develop datasets, computational methods, AI models, and intelligent applications that understand, analyze, and generate human language, and we take them beyond the lab through prototypes, stakeholder collaboration, and deployment.

Our work focuses on **low-resource and multilingual settings**, with a strong interest in Indian languages.

## Our approach

Societal Problem → Language Data → Research → Intelligent Systems → Applications → Deployment → Impact

## Research, Build, Impact

- **Research:** new methods, datasets, models, and evaluation approaches in NLP and language intelligence.
- **Build:** prototypes, platforms, tools, and applications.
- **Impact:** evaluation and deployment with communities and institutions.
